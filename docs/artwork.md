# KAIZEN artwork

Created using the built-in imagegen tool. The originals remain in Codex's generated-images directory. Optimized WebP copies are included in this project:

- `public/kaizen-panorama.webp` — header scenery.
- `public/kaizen-worlds.webp` — Nordic, fortress, and wisteria scenes, arranged in equal thirds for CSS background positioning. Used by cards and the sidebar.

## Panorama prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production website background illustration, extra wide panoramic landscape 3:1. Dark painterly anime fantasy scenery with detailed hand painted ink textures: immense medieval stone fortress wall across a valley below snowy jagged mountains and overcast silver clouds. At the right third, an anonymous dark-haired cloaked traveler seated on a rock, seen from behind, looking toward the fortress. Hanging purple wisteria frames the top right, tiny distant birds. Desaturated charcoal, warm gray, muted parchment highlights, dusty violet. Cinematic and solemn. Keep left third relatively low contrast for a text overlay. Landscape fills entire image edge to edge. NO text, letters, logos, interface, watermarks, panels, or borders. Asset is artwork only, not a UI mockup.

## Card artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative background artwork sprite for a dark anime Kanban app. Wide 3:1 image composed of three equal-width square illustrations side by side, exact thirds, with no gutters, no frames and no text. LEFT THIRD: Vinland Saga inspired rugged Nordic voyage, Viking longship with cream sail on a dark cold sea below distant snowy fjords, charcoal and warm parchment. CENTER THIRD: Attack on Titan inspired immense weathered stone defensive walls and watchtower beneath snowy jagged mountains, tiny dark-cloaked traveler from behind in lower corner, gray stone with muted bronze. RIGHT THIRD: Demon Slayer inspired moonlit Japanese torii gate and distant pagoda under cascading purple wisteria blossoms, dusty violet and ink black, a tiny distant swordsman silhouette. All three scenes share exquisite painterly anime background style, weathered ink texture, desaturated restrained colors and atmospheric light, detailed scenic illustration. No recognizable named characters required, no typography, interface, logos, symbols, panels or watermarks. These are scenic card background assets, not a UI screenshot.
